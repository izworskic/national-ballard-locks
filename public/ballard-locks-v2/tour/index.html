Route lines are orientation aids on an active federal navigation facility. Follow posted signs, closures and staff direction. AIS does not show every boat.

What to understand as you walk

Navigation

Two chambers, different scales

The small chamber handles much of the recreational traffic; the large chamber accommodates much larger vessels and tow configurations.

Water control

Freshwater above Puget Sound

The Locks are also part of the system that holds the Lake Washington Ship Canal near its operating elevation while limiting saltwater intrusion.

Fish passage

Migration around the barrier

The fish ladder lets migrating salmon gain elevation gradually, with an underwater viewing room that becomes much more valuable during active runs.

Research basis

Current operating, access and facility information is grounded in the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. Salmon data comes from WDFW; live vessels come from AIS. Historic/public-art and neighborhood stops are used as visitor context, but the present BallardLocks.org website is not used as an operational authority.
